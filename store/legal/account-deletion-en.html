Account and data deletion

Effective date: 29 Oct 2026 · Contact: danpas@st.amu.edu.pl

1. How to delete in the app

Every signed-in user can request deletion of their own bApka account:

  • Caretaker: Settings → “Delete my account” (type: delete).
  • Senior: info icon next to log out → “Delete my account” (type: delete my account).
  • A caretaker may also delete a paired senior’s account or only unpair without deleting the senior account.

2. What we delete

Account data, caretaker–senior links, and care data tied to the account (medications, schedules, logs, mood, vitals, SOS), unless still required for another active account.

3. Timeframe

Usually immediately after in-app confirmation; at latest within 30 days. After that the action is irreversible.

4. Unpairing

A caretaker can permanently unpair (type: disconnect) — both accounts remain; caretaker access to that senior’s care data ends until you pair again.
