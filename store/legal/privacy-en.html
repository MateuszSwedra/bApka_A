Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 June 2026 · Contact: danpas@st.amu.edu.pl

Protecting your privacy is our priority. We use your data only to operate the bApka app and where required by law.

Data controllers: Daniel Pasternak, Dominika Wabich, Mateusz Swędra, Aleksandra Nowak.

1. What we collect

  • Health-related data (medication schedules, mood, optional vitals, SOS events) — with explicit consent.
  • Account data — email, hashed password or Google ID, display name, role.
  • Push tokens — medication reminders and SOS alerts.

2. Protection

Production traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are hashed. We do not sell personal or health data.

3. Sharing

A paired caretaker can access their dependent’s care data. Cloud processors (database, Expo/Firebase push) act as processors under contract.

4. SOS

SOS notifies paired caretakers. It does not replace emergency numbers (112). Android may require full-screen notification permission.

5. Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, withdraw consent — contact danpas@st.amu.edu.pl. You may lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.
